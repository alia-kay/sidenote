SIDENOTE - TESTER GUIDE (Mac)
Version 2026-11-12 v10

Sidenote is a small window that sits next to your meeting. It reads the
meeting captions and lets you ask Claude about the meeting while it happens.
Meeting notes are saved only on your Mac, in Documents/Sidenote.

You need: a Mac with the latest macOS, Google Chrome, the Claude desktop app
(signed in), an internet connection, and the file
Sidenote-2026-11-12-v10.mcpb.


BEFORE YOU START

1. Nothing else to install. Claude installs everything Sidenote needs
   (including Python) when you install the extension.


INSTALL

2. Double-click Sidenote-2026-11-12-v10.mcpb. Claude opens and shows the
   Sidenote extension. Click Install.
   (Or open Claude > Settings > Extensions and drag the file into the window.)

3. If Claude warns that the extension is not from a verified developer,
   choose to install it anyway. The first install can take 1-2 minutes
   while Claude downloads what Sidenote needs.

4. Quit Claude completely with Cmd+Q, then open it again.


FIRST START

5. Start a normal new chat in Claude and type:
       open Sidenote
   If Claude asks for permission to use Sidenote's tools, choose
   "Always allow".

6. macOS asks for two permissions. Please allow both:
   a. Reading other apps' windows (needed to read the captions):
      open System Settings > Privacy & Security > Device Control and Data
      Access (called "Accessibility" on older macOS) and turn on Claude.
   b. "Claude would like to access files in your Documents folder":
      click Allow (meeting notes are saved in Documents/Sidenote).

7. After turning on Claude in step 6a, quit Claude with Cmd+Q, open it
   again, and type "open Sidenote" in a chat once more. The Sidenote window
   appears next to your screen.
   The first time, Sidenote may ask at the top of its window: "What name
   should Sidenote use for you?". Check the name and click Save (it is
   used instead of "You" in the notes, and never asked again).


TRY IT IN A GOOGLE MEET CALL

8. Join a Google Meet call in Chrome (a call with a friend or colleague,
   or a test call on your own). In the Sidenote window click "Start
   meeting", then talk for a few minutes.
   - Turn on captions in Meet (the CC button). Sidenote tries to turn them
     on by itself.
   - Ask Claude something in the box at the bottom of the Sidenote window,
     for example "What should I ask now?". Your question and Claude's
     answer appear at the top; older ones are below (scroll down).
   - Click "What did I miss?".
   - After about 3 minutes Sidenote should give the meeting a title and
     1-2 agenda items by itself.
   - Open the Agenda, Actions and Transcript views with the buttons at
     the top. Each action item shows its owner, e.g. "Check staging
     (Leon)". If it says "Owner: ?", click it and pick a name.
   - At the end, click "End meeting". Claude writes the recap: a short
     summary, one line per person, decisions, action items and open
     questions. Open the meeting file (... > Open meetings folder) and
     check whether you could forward it as is.

8b. Your microphone (in Zoom, Teams, or just a YouTube video):
   - Close the Google Meet tab, then type "open Sidenote" in a new Claude
     chat. Sidenote uses macOS Live Captions for the other people and your
     microphone for your own voice.
   - If macOS asks "Claude would like to access the microphone", click
     Allow. The first time, the bottom of the Sidenote window says
     "preparing mic" for a minute or two (one-time download).
   - Then the bottom line should say "Mac captions + your mic". Talk for a
     bit and open the Transcript view: your sentences should appear under
     your name.
   - If the bottom line says "Mic blocked" or "Mic gives only silence",
     click it, turn on Claude in the Microphone list, and take a
     screenshot for me.


8c. Zoom in the browser:
   - Join a Zoom meeting in Chrome (on the Zoom invite page choose "Join
     from your browser"). Keep the Zoom tab in front: browsers only let
     Sidenote read the tab on screen.
   - Type "open Sidenote" in a Claude chat. Within a few seconds Sidenote
     turns on Zoom's captions by itself (More > Show Captions) and the
     bottom line says "Zoom captions". Talk a little and check the
     Transcript view. If someone else joins, check whether names appear.
   - If it doesn't work, while captions are on screen type in the Claude
     chat:  capture the Zoom window  and send me the file it saves
     (Documents/Sidenote/capture_zoom_mac.txt).

8d. Microsoft Teams (the Teams app, and Teams in Chrome if you can):
   - Join a Teams meeting and type "open Sidenote" in a Claude chat. Within
     a few seconds Sidenote turns on Teams' live captions by itself (More >
     Language and speech) and the bottom line says "Microsoft Teams
     captions". Talk a little: your lines should appear under your name.
     (Teams may also open its "Caption styles" panel the first time; that
     comes from Teams, you can close it.)
   - If it doesn't work, while captions are on screen type in the Claude
     chat:  capture the Teams window  and send me the file it saves
     (Documents/Sidenote/capture_teams_mac.txt). It contains the text shown
     in the Teams window (captions, names, and chats visible in that window),
     nothing from other windows.


8e. Past meetings (the clock icon at the top):
   - After a meeting or two, click the clock icon. Pick a meeting on the
     left, tick an action item, and ask in the box at the bottom: "What are
     my open action items?". Then close the Sidenote window and ask the same
     in a normal Claude chat.


CHECK THE VERSION

9. In the Claude chat, ask:
       What's Sidenote's status?
   The answer ends with "Version: 2026-11-12 v10". The Sidenote window title
   shows the same version.


IF THE SIDENOTE WINDOW DOES NOT APPEAR

10. Quit Claude with Cmd+Q. Open Terminal (Applications > Utilities >
    Terminal), type this line and press Return:
        sudo pkill -f sidenote.py
    Type your Mac password and press Return (the letters don't show while
    you type; that's normal). Then open Claude again and type
    "open Sidenote" in a chat.
    If it still does not appear, take a screenshot of the Claude chat and
    send it to me.


UNINSTALL

11. In Claude, open Settings > Extensions, choose Sidenote and uninstall it.
    If you want, also delete the folder Documents/Sidenote, and turn Claude
    off again in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Device Control and
    Data Access.


WHAT TO SEND ME

12. Please send back:
    - Screenshots of the Sidenote window during the meeting (and of any
      error or warning you saw while installing).
    - The status line: Claude's answer to "What's Sidenote's status?", and
      the small line at the bottom of the Sidenote window.
    - Anything that was confusing, slow or did not work as you expected.

Thank you for testing Sidenote!
